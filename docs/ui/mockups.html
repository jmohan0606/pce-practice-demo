Sharing a Client Fee Discount
Conflicts with v4
When a client pays more than 10% below the standard fee, the advisor's payout grid moves down one point for every 1% below that threshold. The grid never falls more than 10 points. Applies to managed accounts only, and only where the pricing decision was made on or after 1 Apr 2026.
Example — 115 bps standard, 100 bps actual is a 13% reduction, so the grid moves down 3 points.
Technical detail 
population  managed_platform_cd IS NOT NULL
            AND pricing_decision_dt >= '2026-04-01'
compute     round((standard_bps - client_bps) / standard_bps * 100)
trigger     value > 10
attribute   grid_points = min(value - 10, 10)

Net New Money Award
Draft
An advisor qualifies for the award once total net new money for the year reaches $4 million. The award is the net new money multiplied by the award rate and the advisor's effective grid rate.
Technical detail 
population  advisor_annual_nnm >= 4000000
compute     annual_nnm * award_rate * effective_grid_rate

Six Months After an Advisor Leaves
Draft
Fee discount sharing pauses for six months after the advisor who originally set the discount leaves the firm, then resumes automatically.
Technical detail 
population  departed_advisor_sid IS NOT NULL
            AND months_since(departed_advisor_to_time) <= 6
attribute   suppress FEE_REDUCTION_SHARING
