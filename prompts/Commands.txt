Verify the backend took it:

cmd
curl -s http://localhost:8003/api/health

Deleting documents
-----------------
cmd
curl -s http://localhost:8003/api/documents

That lists them with their IDs, and you can delete directly:

cmd
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:8003/api/documents/<document_id>

Check and delete chromadb
-----------------------------------
Check first, one command after deleting a document:

cmd
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -c "import chromadb,os; c=chromadb.PersistentClient(path=os.getenv('CHROMA_PATH','./chroma')); [print(x.name, x.count()) for x in c.list_collections()]"

Run it before and after a delete. If the count drops, cleanup works and you needn't touch Chroma.

If it doesn't drop, deleting the whole store is the blunt fix:

cmd
rmdir /s /q chroma
